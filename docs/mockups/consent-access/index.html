Things to try

  • Pick Control devices: a short note about actions appears under it.
  • Open Choose in detail, start from Administer, then set Cameras and images to None and Dashboards to Read. This is the example from the design issue: the choice becomes Custom and the notes under Cameras, Dashboards and System appear.
  • Go Back to levels: the choice is kept as a fifth option, Custom, with a short summary.
  • Set an area to None while Entities and their states is at Full, or Templates to Read while Entities is None, to see the other limits.
  • Switch the situation to Household member with limits: Administer is locked, Config files and AppDaemon apps are not available, and Full is locked on Diagnostics and Integrations for two different reasons.
  • Switch to Reconnecting: the choice from last time is preselected and marked.
